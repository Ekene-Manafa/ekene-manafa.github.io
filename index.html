The Story

Not just a CV.
A track record.

Most people stumble into customer service. I chose it, and then I mastered it. Over six years inside Globacom, one of West Africa's largest telecommunications providers, I built a career that most people would need two companies to piece together: frontline agent, email strategist, team coordinator, and escalation specialist. Each role was earned, none handed to me.

I am the person companies call when a client is about to leave, when a regulatory body is asking questions nobody knows how to answer, or when a team has stopped performing and no one can figure out why. I have been in all three situations. I know how to turn things around.

What I bring to a role is not just experience. It is operational maturity. The ability to work with urgency without noise. To communicate clearly across time zones. To own outcomes, not just tasks. And to make clients feel genuinely looked after, not just processed.
